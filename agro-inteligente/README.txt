AGRO-INTELIGENTE (Prototype + Demo)
===================================

HOW TO OPEN
1) Unzip the file (do not open it from inside the ZIP).
2) In the unzipped folder, open a terminal and run:  python -m http.server 8000
3) In Chrome or Firefox, go to:  http://localhost:8000
   (GPS and the map work this way. Internet is needed for the satellite map.)

BUTTONS AT THE TOP
- Prototype: explains that this version is our initial project proposal.
- Demo: opens the 3D demo (folder "demo/") on top of the app, with simulated data.
  Use "Back to the prototype" to return without losing anything you entered.

GOOGLE EARTH
- "Open in Google Earth" opens your location there.
- Draw your land in Google Earth, save it as KML and use "Import KML".
- At the end, "View my land in Google Earth (KML)" downloads your land as a KML file.

PHOTOS
- Crop and soil photos are loaded from Wikipedia (internet needed).
- To use your own, put .jpg files in "fotos/" (names in fotos/README.txt). They take priority.
