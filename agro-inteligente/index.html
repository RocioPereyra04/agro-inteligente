🧪 This is a prototype

This version is our initial proposal for the project. It shows the design and the features we want to develop, such as:

  • 📍 Mark your land on a satellite map (search, GPS and Google Earth import)
  • 🌍 Describe your soil and your water
  • 🌿 Register the plants you already grow, with photos
  • 💡 Get crop suggestions based on climate, season and soil
  • ✅ Review and edit your information

It is not the finished app: some parts are simplified and everything may change as the project grows. To see how the final app would look and work, press the Demo button.
